This R Shiny dashboard is designed for a clinical comparative research study between the Smart Eye Camera and the conventional Slit Lamp. The application allows eye care professionals to record patient data, compare clinical measurements obtained from both devices (such as vision, IOP, and refraction), and collect feedback on technical and usability parameters. The dashboard includes features for data entry, real-time visualization, statistical tests (paired t-test, McNemar test, Cohen's Kappa, Wilcoxon signed-rank), and automated generation of research tables. This tool helps researchers make evidence-based decisions about how accurate, efficient, and user-friendly the Smart Eye Camera is compared to the traditional Slit Lamp, and the final results can be exported in CSV or Excel format for use in publications or reports.
